🔍 Backfill Audit

Re-checks every backfilled (missed-day) report. For each one it works out what was really in the fridge that day — the merchandiser's last live report plus supplies and stock corrections up to that day — and compares it with the sales she reported. Anything sold above that amount is flagged. Reports made before the new cap rule will show up here if they over-reported. If a flagged day turns out to be legitimate, tap Mark as checked and give a reason — it is saved and no longer counted.
